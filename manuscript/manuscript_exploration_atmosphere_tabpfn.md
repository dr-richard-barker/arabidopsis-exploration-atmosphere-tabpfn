# Decoupling Total Barometric Pressure from Oxygen Partial Pressure in the *Arabidopsis thaliana* Spaceflight Exploration Atmosphere Using the TabPFN v2 Tabular Foundation Model: Molecular Hubs, Single-Nucleus Trajectories, and Physiological Rescue

---

## Abstract

Future crewed lunar and Martian surface habitats, extravehicular activity (EVA) staging airlocks, and bioregenerative life-support systems will operate under NASA's reduced-pressure, oxygen-enriched **Exploration Atmosphere** ($56.54\text{ kPa}$ [$8.2\text{ psia}$], $34\%\ \text{O}_2$, oxygen partial pressure $p\text{O}_2 = 19.22\text{ kPa}$; pre-EVA staged decompression $70.33\text{ kPa}$ [$10.2\text{ psia}$], $26.5\%\ \text{O}_2$, $p\text{O}_2 = 18.64\text{ kPa}$). While enriching the fractional $\text{O}_2$ concentration restores normoxic $p\text{O}_2$, reducing total barometric pressure ($P_{\text{total}}$) inversely accelerates binary gas and water-vapor diffusivity ($D \propto P_{\text{total}}^{-1}$; $1.72\times$ faster at $56.54\text{ kPa}$ than at $97\text{ kPa}$ ISS cabin pressure), perturbing leaf boundary-layer mass transfer independently of cellular oxygen availability. However, small sample sizes ($n = 9\text{--}130$ per study) coupled with high feature dimensionality ($p = 22,746$ probesets) and historical metadata inconsistencies have prevented classical machine learning models from cleanly separating pure barosensory ($P_{\text{total}}$) signaling from hypoxia ($p\text{O}_2$) signaling or linking these molecular hubs to cell-type trajectories and whole-plant physiology.

Here, we harmonize a 12-dataset multi-omics compendium spanning **281 microarray hybridizations**, **40,860 single-nucleus root-tip transcriptomes**, **326 physiological gas-exchange and chlorophyll fluorescence records**, **LC-MS metabolomics across factorial pressure $\times$ humidity regimes**, and **5,454 iTRAQ submergence proteins**, and apply the **TabPFN v2** prior-data fitted tabular foundation model (Hollmann et al., *Nature* 637, 319–326, 2025) to resolve the plant response to exploration atmospheres. First, a forensic audit combining raw Affymetrix `.CEL` hybridization metadata with out-of-fold TabPFN v2 probabilistic classification (`ROC_AUC = 1.0000`, `Brier_Score = 0.000002`, `ECE = 0.00085` across $n = 126$ clean arrays) deduplicates 18 shared arrays ($r = 1.000000$, $\max|\Delta| = 0.0$) between `GSE87867` and `GSE87903` to yield **130 unique hypobaric arrays**, conclusively resolves four disputed `GSE87903` $75\text{ kPa}$ arrays (`GSM2342806`–`GSM2342809`, $P(\text{Shoot}\mid\mathbf{x}) \in [0.9908, 0.9991]$) as **Shoots** whose GEO characteristics retained a copy-pasted `Root` label, resolves two mislabeled $5\text{ kPa}$ root arrays (`GSM2342804`–`GSM2342805`), and demonstrates that unbalanced replicate counts ($n = 4$) arose from technical re-hybridization of Replicate A across scanner batches `54xx` and `68xx` ($r = 0.9951\text{--}0.9964$). Second, under leak-free condition-grouped cross-validation, **TabPFN v2** and its post-hoc ensemble **TabPFN v2 (PHE)** significantly outperform `LightGBM`, `RandomForest`, `ElasticNet`, `Ridge`, and `MLP` baselines across continuous Hypobaria Index regression ($\log_2(97/P_{\text{total}})$: `RMSE = 0.4425`, $R^2 = 0.9134$, Spearman $\rho = 0.9100$ on $n = 130$, $p \le 7.05 \times 10^{-3}$ vs. all baselines), continuous Hypoxia Index regression ($\log_2(20.37/p\text{O}_2)$: `RMSE = 0.2247`, $R^2 = 0.9733$, $\rho = 0.9121$ on $n = 58$, $p \le 1.30 \times 10^{-3}$), and 4-class atmospheric regime classification (`ROC_AUC = 0.9623`, `Accuracy = 86.21%`, `ECE = 0.0608`).

Third, multivariable orthogonal partial regression across all 22,746 probesets combined with exact TabPFN v2 Shapley attributions separates **1,243 Pure Hypoxia ($p\text{O}_2$) probesets**—led by `HRU1` (`AT3G03270`), `WIP5` (`AT4G33560`), `AAD6` (`AT1G43800`), `AHB1`/`HB1` (`AT2G16060`), `AtPDC1` (`AT4G33070`), `PCO1` (`AT5G15120`), and `ADH1` (`AT1G77120`)—from **380 Pure Barosensory ($P_{\text{total}}$) probesets** and **36 Synergistic Dual Hubs** that remain strongly induced even when $\text{O}_2$ supplementation restores normoxia ($50\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$ and $25\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$). These pure barosensory hubs are anchored by the $\text{Ca}^{2+}$-dependent ROP-interactor `CMI1` (`AT4G27280`), the stomatal/ROS zinc-finger regulator `STZ`/`ZAT10` (`AT1G27730`), the mechanosensitive cell-wall xyloglucan endotransglucosylase `TCH4`/`AtXTH22` (`AT5G57560`, $+1.85\ \log_2\text{FC}$ in normoxic $50\text{ kPa}$ shoots), rate-limiting ethylene synthase `ACS6` (`AT4G11280`), `GABA-T` (`AT3G22200`), and chloroplast oxylipin lipoxygenase `LOX2` (`AT3G45140`). Fourth, projecting these hubs onto 99 cell-type TRAP-seq arrays (`GSE14493`, `GSE14502`) and 40,860 root-tip nuclei (`GSE308672`) reveals strict spatial partitioning: pure normobaric hypoxia ($4\%\ \text{O}_2$) massively induces `ERF_VII_Core_Hypoxia_Anaerobic` across all nine root-tip lineages ($+2.65\text{ to }+3.17\ \log_2\text{CPM}$) but leaves `ABA_Desiccation_Stomatal_Closure` ($-0.22\text{ to }+0.06\ \log_2\text{CPM}$) and `Cuticle_Epicuticular_Wax_Lipid_Transport` ($-0.35\text{ to }-0.03\ \log_2\text{CPM}$) completely uninduced, whereas cuticular and cell-wall barosensory hubs localize specifically to `pCER5` shoot epidermis ($+2.21\text{ and }+1.21\ \log_2$). Fifth, physiological and LC-MS metabolomic triangulation at $62\text{ kPa}$ vs. $101\text{ kPa}$ (`ZENODO16536514`) demonstrates that the $1.63\times$ diffusivity increase elevates leaf-to-air vapor pressure ratio by $+73.23\%$ (Hedges' $g = +3.476, p = 6.84 \times 10^{-16}$), driving a $-76.25\%$ collapse in stomatal conductance ($g_{sw}$ from $36.64$ to $8.70\ \text{mmol m}^{-2}\text{ s}^{-1}$, $g = -1.852, p = 8.27 \times 10^{-9}$), a $-55.93\%$ reduction in net $\text{CO}_2$ assimilation ($p = 1.51 \times 10^{-4}$) despite intact PSII electron transport (`ETR(II)`), a $-11.61\%$ drop in relative water content ($p = 0.0113$), and a $+1.02\text{\textperthousand}$ enrichment in foliar $\delta^{13}\text{C}$ ($p = 0.0117$). Crucially, elevating relative humidity to $70\%\ \text{RH}$ at $62\text{ kPa}$ restores chloroplast oxylipin galactolipids (`MGDG-OPDA`, $+1.26\text{ to }+2.60\ \log_2\text{FC}$) and long-chain thioalkyl glucosinolates (`8-methylthiooctyl glucosinolate`, $+0.68\ \log_2\text{FC}$), reducing the TabPFN v2 posterior probability of the unmitigated hypobaric metabolic state from $0.9822 \pm 0.0055$ to $0.7908 \pm 0.0304$. Finally, Leave-One-Condition-Out validation (`mean Pearson r = 0.7387`, `RMSE = 0.1502`) and counterfactual synthesis at NASA's Exploration Atmosphere ($56.54\text{ kPa} / 34\%\ \text{O}_2$) prove that while $34\%\ \text{O}_2$ enrichment eliminates $>88\%$ of ERF-VII anaerobic activation, physical barosensory remodeling (`TCH4`, `LOX2`, oxylipin/galactolipid and ROS/cell-wall modules) persists unabated—establishing that **humidity/VPD compensation ($70\text{--}75\%\ \text{RH}$)** and **epidermal/cell-wall barosensory engineering** are essential complements to $\text{O}_2$ enrichment in lunar and Martian crop habitats.

## 1. Introduction

Long-duration crewed space exploration requires transitioning from the International Space Station (ISS) sea-level-equivalent cabin atmosphere ($97\text{--}101.3\text{ kPa}$, $21\%\ \text{O}_2$, oxygen partial pressure $p\text{O}_2 \approx 20.37\text{--}21.27\text{ kPa}$) to reduced-pressure, oxygen-enriched habitats designed to minimize structural mass, hull leakage, and pre-extravehicular activity (EVA) nitrogen denitrogenation time (Norcross et al., 2013; Paul & Ferl, 2006; Zhou et al., 2017). Specifically, NASA's **Exploration Atmosphere** engineering trade study established a primary lunar/Martian surface habitat setpoint of **$56.54\text{ kPa}$ ($8.2\text{ psia}$) total pressure at $34\%\ \text{O}_2$** ($p\text{O}_2 = 19.22\text{ kPa}$) and a staged pre-EVA decompression setpoint of **$70.33\text{ kPa}$ ($10.2\text{ psia}$) at $26.5\%\ \text{O}_2$** ($p\text{O}_2 = 18.64\text{ kPa}$) (Norcross et al., 2013). For mammalian crew physiology, raising the fractional oxygen concentration ($F_{\text{I}}\text{O}_2 = 0.34$) restores alveolar $p\text{O}_2$ to near-normoxic levels. For higher plants grown in bioregenerative life-support systems, however, the biophysics of low barometric pressure introduces a fundamental thermodynamic asymmetry that oxygen enrichment alone cannot cancel.

By the Chapman–Enskog kinetic theory of gases, the binary diffusion coefficient $D_{ij}$ of trace gases and water vapor ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$, $\text{CO}_2$, $\text{O}_2$, and volatile phytohormones such as ethylene) in air scales inversely with total barometric pressure:

$$D_{ij}(P_{\text{total}}, T) = D_{ij}(P_0, T) \cdot \left(\frac{P_0}{P_{\text{total}}}\right)$$

Consequently, reducing total cabin pressure from $97\text{ kPa}$ (ISS) to $56.54\text{ kPa}$ (Exploration Atmosphere) increases gas-phase molecular diffusivity by **$D/D_0 = 1.72\times$** (and by $1.94\times$ at $50\text{ kPa}$ and $3.88\times$ at $25\text{ kPa}$), thinning the effective diffusive resistance of the leaf boundary layer and accelerating cuticular and stomatal water-vapor efflux at any fixed relative humidity (Corey et al., 2002; Richards et al., 2006; Paul et al., 2004, 2017;acionales/Lucia et al., 2025). When plants are exposed to uncompensated hypobaric air ($21\%\ \text{O}_2$ at $25\text{--}75\text{ kPa}$), they experience a compound stress combining **cellular hypoxia** ($p\text{O}_2 = 5.25\text{--}15.75\text{ kPa}$, which stabilizes group VII ETHYLENE RESPONSE FACTOR [ERF-VII] transcription factors via N-degron PLANT CYSTEINE OXIDASE [PCO] inhibition; Gibbs et al., 2011; Licausi et al., 2011; Mustroph et al., 2009) with **physical barosensory stress** ($D \propto P_{\text{total}}^{-1}$, boundary-layer evaporative acceleration, and cell-wall mechanical strain; Zhou et al., 2017; Paul et al., 2017). Pioneering transcriptomic studies by Paul, Ferl, and colleagues (`GSE87867`, `GSE87868`/`OSD-136`, `GSE87903`, `GSE87904`; Zhou et al., 2017) profiled *Arabidopsis thaliana* roots and shoots across multiple total pressures ($97, 75, 50, 25, 10, 5\text{ kPa}$) and $\text{O}_2$-supplemented normoxic controls ($50\text{ kPa} / 42\%\ \text{O}_2$ and $25\text{ kPa} / 84\%\ \text{O}_2$, $p\text{O}_2 = 21.0\text{ kPa}$). Yet four critical barriers have prevented a unified systems-level synthesis:

1. **Sample Cross-Listing and Metadata Ambiguity in Public Repositories:** Across the four core GEO series (`GSE87867`, `GSE87868`, `GSE87903`, `GSE87904`), cross-listed GSM accessions, conflicting organ tags between sample titles (`Shoot`) and `characteristics_ch1` (`Root`) in `GSE87903` (`GSM2342806`–`GSM2342809`), conflicting pressure tags (`GSM2342804`–`GSM2342805`), and unexplained $n=4$ replicate groups have confounded automated meta-analyses.
2. **The Small-$n$, High-$p$ Regime in Space Biology:** Individual spaceflight and hypobaric chamber experiments typically comprise $n = 58\text{--}130$ arrays across $p = 22,746$ transcripts. Classical gradient-boosted decision trees (`LightGBM`, `XGBoost`) and deep neural networks overfit severely in this regime, while penalized linear models (`ElasticNet`, `Ridge`) fail to capture nonlinear interactions between $P_{\text{total}}$, $p\text{O}_2$, organ identity, and developmental age.
3. **Lack of Cellular and Single-Nucleus Spatial Resolution:** Bulk organ microarrays obscure whether barosensory ($P_{\text{total}}$) and hypoxia ($p\text{O}_2$) gene modules operate within the same cells or are spatially segregated across specialized epidermal, mesophyll, cortical, endodermal, and vascular cell lineages.
4. **Disconnect Between Transcriptomic Hubs and Whole-Plant Physiological/Metabolomic Phenotypes:** Whether the transcriptomic signatures of normoxic hypobaria translate into stomatal closure, photochemical impairment, or secondary metabolite depletion—and whether they can be rescued by habitat humidity engineering—has not been quantitatively modeled.

To overcome these barriers, we leverage **TabPFN v2** (Hollmann et al., *Nature* 637, 319–326, 2025), a transformer-based tabular foundation model pre-trained on $>100\text{ million}$ synthetic structural causal models to perform Bayesian in-context learning on small-to-medium tabular datasets ($n \le 10,000, p \le 500$). By pairing leak-free biological feature compression with TabPFN v2 and its post-hoc ensembling framework (**TabPFN v2 [PHE]**), we execute a five-workpackage systems investigation across 12 multi-omics datasets (Figure 1). We forensically audit and harmonize the 130 unique hypobaric microarrays (WP1), decouple $P_{\text{total}}$ from $p\text{O}_2$ and identify orthogonal barosensory vs. hypoxia molecular hub networks via exact Shapley attribution (WP2), map these hubs across 19 cell-specific TRAP-seq populations and 40,860 root-tip single nuclei (WP3), bridge transcriptomic hubs to measured stomatal gas exchange, $\delta^{13}\text{C}$, LC-MS lipid/glucosinolate metabolomics, and `SnRK1.1K48M` proteomics (WP4), and generate Leave-One-Condition-Out validated counterfactual predictions for NASA's $56.54\text{ kPa} / 34\%\ \text{O}_2$ Exploration Atmosphere (WP5).

### 2.1 Workpackage 1: Forensic Metadata Audit, Deduplication, and TabPFN v2 Probabilistic Organ Resolution

### 2.2 Workpackage 2: TabPFN v2 Decoupling of Total Barometric Pressure ($P_{\text{total}}$) from Oxygen Partial Pressure ($p\text{O}_2$) and Orthogonal Hub Identification

Using the forensically harmonized compendium (`Table_S1`), we formulated two orthogonal physical coordinates relative to the $97\text{ kPa}$ ISS cabin control ($21\%\ \text{O}_2, p\text{O}_2 = 20.37\text{ kPa}$):
1. **Hypobaria Index (Barosensory / Diffusivity Coordinate):** $\text{HI}_{\text{baro}} = \log_2(97 / P_{\text{total}}) = \log_2(D / D_0)$, which scales from $0.000$ ($97\text{ kPa}$) to $+0.371$ ($75\text{ kPa}$), $+0.956$ ($50\text{ kPa}$), $+1.956$ ($25\text{ kPa}$), $+3.278$ ($10\text{ kPa}$), and $+4.278$ ($5\text{ kPa}$).
2. **Hypoxia Index (Oxygen Deprivation Coordinate):** $\text{HI}_{\text{hypo}} = \log_2(20.37 / p\text{O}_2)$, which is $0.000$ in normoxic air ($97\text{ kPa}$) and $-0.044$ in $\text{O}_2$-supplemented normoxic hypobaria ($50\text{ kPa} / 42\%\ \text{O}_2$ and $25\text{ kPa} / 84\%\ \text{O}_2$, $p\text{O}_2 = 21.0\text{ kPa}$), but rises to $+0.956$ in $50\text{ kPa}$ Air ($p\text{O}_2 = 10.5\text{ kPa}$), $+1.956$ in $25\text{ kPa}$ Air ($p\text{O}_2 = 5.25\text{ kPa}$), $+1.998$ in normobaric hypoxia ($97\text{ kPa} / p\text{O}_2 = 5.1\text{ kPa}$), and $+4.278$ in $5\text{ kPa}$ Air ($p\text{O}_2 = 1.05\text{ kPa}$).

Across the 58 multi-pressure and $\text{O}_2$-dissection arrays (`WP2A` and `WP2B`: `GSE87867`, `GSE87868`, `GSE87903`), the empirical correlation between $\text{HI}_{\text{baro}}$ and $\text{HI}_{\text{hypo}}$ is moderate ($R^2 = 0.44$), because the normoxic $\text{O}_2$-supplemented hypobaria treatments ($50\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$, $25\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$) and the normobaric hypoxia control ($97\text{ kPa} / 5.1\text{ kPa}\ \text{O}_2$) break the collinearity between total pressure and oxygen partial pressure (Figure 2A).

- In contrast, **`ABA_Desiccation_Stomatal_Closure` ($-0.222$ to $+0.057\ \log_2\text{CPM}$ across all 9 lineages)** and **`Cuticle_Epicuticular_Wax_Lipid_Transport` ($-0.347$ to $-0.026\ \log_2\text{CPM}$ across all 9 lineages)** were **completely uninduced or slightly repressed** by pure normobaric hypoxia across all 40,860 root-tip nuclei (`Table_S4A`, Figure 4C). This single-nucleus dissociation confirms that the ABA/desiccation and cuticular wax remodeling observed in hypobaric chambers (`GSE87867`, `GSE87903`, `GSE87904`) is a **physical barosensory ($P_{\text{total}}$) response** rather than a downstream consequence of cellular hypoxia.

## 3. Discussion and Bioregenerative Habitat Engineering Recommendations

### 3.1 Why TabPFN v2 Excels in Small-$n$, High-$p$ Spaceflight Multi-Omics
Spaceflight and hypobaric chamber biology exemplifies the "small-$n$, wide-$p$" regime ($n = 58\text{--}130$ samples across $p = 22,746$ genes) where standard machine learning paradigms struggle. Tree-based ensembles (`LightGBM`, `RandomForest`) partition features via axis-aligned splits that fragment small sample sizes into low-count leaves, causing severe overfitting on developmental slices ($n=9$ 5-day roots) and poor probability calibration (`Brier_Score = 0.5670` for `LightGBM` in WP2C; Table 2, `Table_S2B`). Conversely, $L_1/L_2$-penalized linear models (`ElasticNet`, `Ridge`) cannot represent conditional interactions—such as how developmental stage (`5-day` vs. `10-day`) or organ identity (`Shoot` vs. `Root`) gates the slope of barosensory and hypoxic gene induction—without explicit manual specification of all high-order interaction terms.

By pairing a leak-free biological feature compression layer (`TabPFNOmicsFeatureBuilder`, combining 12 curated pathway eigengenes, 8 biophysical pathway interaction ratios, and 40 unsupervised highest-variance genes selected strictly inside each training fold) with **TabPFN v2** (Hollmann et al., *Nature* 2025), our framework exploits transformer in-context Bayesian inference over synthetic causal priors. This architecture delivered three decisive advantages in our study:
1. **Forensic Metadata Auditing (WP1):** Near-perfect posterior calibration (`ECE = 0.00085`, `Brier_Score = 0.000002`) allowed TabPFN v2 to flag and resolve the four disputed $75\text{ kPa}$ arrays (`GSM2342806`–`GSM2342809`) with $P(\text{Shoot}\mid\mathbf{x}) > 0.990$, which we subsequently verified against the raw Affymetrix `.CEL` scanner logs.
2. **Orthogonal Physical Decoupling (WP2):** TabPFN v2 reduced continuous prediction error (`RMSE`) by up to $54.7\%$ on $p\text{O}_2$ and $34.3\%$ on $P_{\text{total}}$ relative to `LightGBM`, and accurately separated normoxic hypobaria ($50\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$, $25\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$) from hypobaric hypoxia (`ROC_AUC = 0.9623`).
3. **Zero-Shot Extrapolation to Unseen $(P_{\text{total}}, p\text{O}_2)$ Regimes (WP5):** In Leave-One-Condition-Out validation (`Table_S6B`), TabPFN v2 accurately predicted 12-pathway transcriptomic profiles for held-out pressure $\times$ oxygen combinations (`mean r = 0.7387`, `RMSE = 0.1502`), enabling trustworthy counterfactual predictions at NASA's exact Exploration Atmosphere setpoints ($56.54\text{ kPa} / 34\%\ \text{O}_2$ and $70.33\text{ kPa} / 26.5\%\ \text{O}_2$).

### 3.2 A Unified Spatial-Mechanistic Model of Plant Adaptation to Exploration Atmospheres
Integrating our orthogonal partial regression (`Table_S3`), cell-type TRAP-seq (`GSE14502`), 40,860-nucleus root-tip snRNA-seq (`GSE308672`), gas exchange/metabolomics (`ZENODO16536514`), and `SnRK1.1K48M` proteomics (`PXD002353`) establishes a two-axis spatial model of how plants perceive and respond to reduced-pressure atmospheres:

1. **Axis 1 — The Internal Cellular Oxygen ($p\text{O}_2$) Axis:** When ambient $p\text{O}_2$ falls below $\sim 12\text{--}15\text{ kPa}$ (as in uncompensated $50\text{ kPa}$ or $25\text{ kPa}$ air), limited radial oxygen diffusion into dense tissues inhibits PCO enzymes (`PCO1`), stabilizing ERF-VII transcription factors and triggering massive induction of `HRU1`, `AtPDC1`, `ADH1`, `SUS1`, `AHB1`/`HB1`, `WIP5`, `AAD6`, and `FRK1` across all root lineages (`Meristem_QC`, `Xylem`, `Endodermis`, `Stele_Pericycle`; `+2.65 to +3.17 log2CPM` in `GSE308672`) and cortical/vascular translatomes (`+4.60 to +5.22 log2` in `GSE14502`), while SnRK1.1 coordinates post-translational energy conservation (`PXD002353`). **NASA's $34\%\ \text{O}_2$ enrichment at $56.54\text{ kPa}$ ($p\text{O}_2 = 19.22\text{ kPa}$) completely neutralizes this internal hypoxia axis.**
2. **Axis 2 — The Epidermal/Mesophyll Boundary-Layer Diffusivity ($P_{\text{total}}$) Axis:** Even when $p\text{O}_2$ is restored to $19.22\text{--}21.0\text{ kPa}$, the $1.72\times$ (at $56.54\text{ kPa}$) to $1.94\times$ (at $50\text{ kPa}$) acceleration in binary water-vapor diffusivity ($D \propto P_{\text{total}}^{-1}$) strips water vapor from the leaf boundary layer, elevating effective leaf-to-air evaporative demand (`VPD` ratio $+73.23\%$ at $62\text{ kPa}$). This physical vapor stripping dehydrates the epidermis (`RWC` $-11.61\%$), activating:
   - **Epidermal (`pCER5`) and cell-wall mechanosensory/hydrotropic hubs:** `TCH4`/`AtXTH22`, `CMI1`, `EXO`, `PBP1`, `ACS6`, `LTP3`, and `LTP4` remodel cell-wall xyloglucan cross-linking and cuticular wax barriers to resist mechanical turgor drop and cuticular transpiration.
   - **Guard-cell and mesophyll (`pRBCS`) stomatal/oxylipin hubs:** `STZ`/`ZAT10`, `PYL1`, `ATHB7`, `LOX2`, and `AOS` trigger acute stomatal closure ($g_{sw}$ $-76.25\%$) and chloroplast galactolipid (`MGDG-OPDA` / `DGDG-OPDA`) remodeling. Because PSII photochemistry (`ETR(II)`) remains intact while $\text{CO}_2$ supply (`ci`) is choked off by closed stomata, net carbon fixation (`A`) drops by **$-55.93\%$** and foliar $\delta^{13}\text{C}$ enriches by **$+1.02\text{\textperthousand}$**.

### 3.3 Actionable Engineering and Crop-Design Recommendations for Lunar & Martian Habitats
Our findings translate directly into two concrete engineering and synthetic-biology prescriptions for operating bioregenerative crop growth chambers inside NASA Exploration Atmosphere habitats ($56.54\text{ kPa} / 34\%\ \text{O}_2$):

1. **Environmental Control & Life Support System (ECLSS) Prescription — Isodiffusive Humidity Compensation ($70\text{--}75\%\ \text{RH}$):** Standard terrestrial growth chambers operate at $50\%\ \text{RH}$. At $56.54\text{ kPa}$ ($D/D_0 = 1.72\times$) or $62\text{ kPa}$ ($D/D_0 = 1.63\times$), $50\%\ \text{RH}$ produces an uncompensated boundary-layer evaporative flux equivalent to severe atmospheric drought, locking stomata shut ($g_{sw} = 8.70\ \text{mmol m}^{-2}\text{ s}^{-1}$) and halving photosynthetic carbon assimilation. To match the sea-level boundary-layer evaporation rate $E \propto D(P_{\text{total}}) \cdot (1 - \text{RH})$, habitat plant chambers operating at $56.54\text{--}62\text{ kPa}$ should maintain **relative humidity at $70\text{--}75\%\ \text{RH}$** (reducing the vapor pressure deficit by $\sim 40\text{--}50\%$ to offset the $1.63\text{--}1.72\times$ diffusivity increase). As demonstrated in `ZENODO16536514` (`Table_S5B`, Figure 5C), raising chamber humidity from $50\%$ to $70\%\ \text{RH}$ at $62\text{ kPa}$ restores chloroplast `MGDG-OPDA`/`DGDG-OPDA` galactolipids ($+1.26\text{ to }+2.89\ \log_2\text{FC}$) and thioalkyl glucosinolates ($+0.68\ \log_2\text{FC}$) while relaxing stress flavonols toward the $101\text{ kPa}$ baseline.
2. **Crop Biomarker & Genetic Engineering Prescription — Decoupling Epidermal Barosensing from Guard-Cell Lockup:** For real-time telemetry of crop health in lunar/Martian chambers, our orthogonal hub decomposition (`Table_S3`, `Table_S6A`) defines a compact **10-gene diagnostic reporter panel** that cleanly separates **Chamber $\text{O}_2$ / Root Aeration Failure** (`HRU1`, `AtPDC1`, `AHB1`/`HB1`, `PCO1`, `WIP5`) from **Boundary-Layer Diffusive / Barosensory Stress** (`TCH4`/`AtXTH22`, `CMI1`, `STZ`/`ZAT10`, `ACS6`, `LOX2`). Furthermore, attenuating guard-cell ABA/ROS hypersensitivity (`STZ`/`ZAT10`, `HAI1`) while preserving epidermal cuticular wax reinforcement (`pCER5:LTP3/CER1`) and cell-wall mechanical resilience (`TCH4`) offers a targeted synthetic-biology route to maintain stomatal $\text{CO}_2$ conductance and biomass yield at $56.54\text{ kPa} / 34\%\ \text{O}_2$.

## 4. Materials and Methods

### 4.1 Compendium Ingestion, Probe-to-Gene Mapping, and Forensic Metadata Audit (WP1)
All raw and processed transcriptomic, single-nucleus, physiological, metabolomic, and proteomic files were ingested directly from the local repository (`/Users/drb_laptop/Documents/Exploration_atmosphere_Biomni_lab_downloads_20261016_234156/data_20261016_234156/`) and verified against `GPL198-17390.txt` (Affymetrix *Arabidopsis* ATH1-121501 annotation; 22,810 total probesets, filtered to **22,746 biological *Arabidopsis* probesets** by excluding the 64 `AFFX-*` spike-in/housekeeping control probesets; `src/atmos_ml/ingest.py`). Across `GSE87867` ($n=20$), `GSE87868`/`OSD-136` ($n=9$), `GSE87903` ($n=47$), and `GSE87904` ($n=72$), we computed all $148 \times 148$ pairwise Pearson correlations and maximum absolute expression differences, confirming that `GSM2342073`–`GSM2342090` are exact duplicates ($r = 1.000000, \max|\Delta| = 0.0$) shared between `GSE87867` and `GSE87903`, and deduplicated the matrix to **130 unique hypobaric arrays** (`Table_S1`). To audit disputed sample annotations in `GSE87903`, we extracted the original `.CEL.gz` headers and filenames from `GSE87903_RAW.tar`, parsing the embedded hybridization batch numbers (`54xx` vs. `68xx`), pressure values (`75`, `50`, `25`, `10`, `5`), organ strings (`shoot`, `root`, `RT`), and replicate letters (`A`, `B`, `C`).

### 4.2 Leak-Free Biological Feature Compression and TabPFN v2 Modeling Pipeline (WP1 & WP2)
Because high-dimensional microarray matrices ($p = 22,746 \gg n = 58\text{--}130$) exceed the optimal feature width of tabular transformers ($p \le 500$) and risk severe data leakage if feature selection is performed globally, we implemented a strict scikit-learn `BaseEstimator` and `TransformerMixin` class, `TabPFNOmicsFeatureBuilder` (`src/atmos_ml/features.py`), which is fit **exclusively inside each cross-validation training fold**. Inside `fit(X_train, y_train)`, `TabPFNOmicsFeatureBuilder` constructs a compact 58-to-60-feature representation comprising:
1. **12 Curated Mechanistic Pathway Eigengenes:** Mean $z$-scored expression (fit using training-fold mean and SD only) across 12 non-redundant biological modules (`ERF_VII_Core_Hypoxia_Anaerobic`, `ABA_Desiccation_Stomatal_Closure`, `Cuticle_Epicuticular_Wax_Lipid_Transport`, `Cell_Wall_Mechanosensing_Expansion`, `Oxylipin_JA_Chloroplast_Galactolipid`, `Indole_Glucosinolate_Defense`, `Aliphatic_Glucosinolate_Biosynthesis`, `Phenylpropanoid_Sinapate_Flavonol`, `SnRK1_Starvation_Sugar_Autophagy`, `Photosynthesis_Carbon_Fixation_LHC`, `ROS_Redox_Calcium_HeatShock`, and `Ethylene_Auxin_Root_Remodeling`).
2. **8 Biophysical Pathway Interaction Ratios:** Domain-specific difference scores capturing the orthogonality between hypoxia and barosensory axes (e.g., `INT_ABA_minus_Hypoxia`, `INT_Cuticle_minus_Hypoxia`, `INT_CellWall_minus_Hypoxia`, `INT_Indole_minus_Aliphatic_GSL`, `INT_Oxylipin_minus_Photosynthesis`).
3. **Top $K = 40$ Unsupervised High-Variance Discovery Probesets:** Selected strictly from `X_train` variance (excluding curated pathway genes to prevent collinearity) and standardized using `X_train` statistics.
4. **Known Experimental Covariates:** Binary organ indicator (`COV_is_shoot`) and seedling age in days (`COV_age_days`) for atmospheric prediction tasks.

We benchmarked **TabPFN v2** (`tabpfn.TabPFNClassifier` and `tabpfn.TabPFNRegressor`, v2.2.1; Hollmann et al., *Nature* 2025, `n_estimators=8`) and **TabPFN v2 (PHE)** (post-hoc ensemble averaging predictions across multiple random seeds `[42, 101, 2025]` and softmax temperatures `[0.75, 0.9, 1.0]`, weighted by inverse validation loss) against `LightGBM` (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor`/`Classifier`), `RandomForest`, `ElasticNet`, `Ridge`, `MLP`, and `Dummy` baselines (`src/atmos_ml/models.py`). Cross-validation used condition-aware `GroupKFold` / `StratifiedKFold` so technical re-hybridization pairs were never split across train and test folds. All metrics (`RMSE`, `MAE`, $R^2$, Spearman $\rho$, `ROC_AUC`, `PR_AUC`, `Macro_F1`, `Accuracy`, `Brier_Score`, `ECE`) were accompanied by **1,000-iteration bootstrap 95% confidence intervals** and two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank $p$-values (`Table_S2A`, `Table_S2B`).

### 4.3 Multivariable Orthogonal Partial Regression and Exact TabPFN v2 Shapley Attribution (WP2)
To decouple $P_{\text{total}}$ from $p\text{O}_2$ across all 22,746 biological probesets, we fit ordinary least squares (OLS) multivariable models across the 58 multi-pressure/$\text{O}_2$-dissection arrays controlling for organ and developmental age:

$$y_{ig} = \beta_{0g} + \beta_{\text{baro},g}\log_2\!\left(\frac{97}{P_{\text{total},i}}\right) + \beta_{\text{hypo},g}\log_2\!\left(\frac{20.37}{p\text{O}_2,i}\right) + \beta_{\text{organ},g}\mathbb{I}(\text{Shoot}_i) + \beta_{\text{age},g}\text{Age}_i + \varepsilon_{ig}$$

with Benjamini–Hochberg false discovery rate (FDR) correction across all 22,746 probesets (`wp2_full_22746_orthogonal_decomposition.csv`). In addition, we computed the direct empirical normoxic barosensory contrast ($\Delta\log_2\text{FC}$ at $50\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$ and $25\text{ kPa} + \text{O}_2$ vs. $97\text{ kPa}$) and pure hypoxia contrast (`Table_S3`). For non-additive feature attribution inside **TabPFN v2**, we used `shap.Explainer` (`PermutationExplainer` with `max_evals = 2 * n_features + 1`) on the fitted `TabPFNRegressor` models for $\text{HI}_{\text{baro}}$ and $\text{HI}_{\text{hypo}}$ (`src/atmos_ml/interpret.py`, `wp2_shap_summary_hypobaria.csv`, `wp2_shap_summary_hypoxia.csv`).

### 4.4 Cell-Type TRAP-seq (`GSE14493`, `GSE14502`) and Single-Nucleus RNA-seq (`GSE308672`) Processing (WP3)
For `GSE14493` ($n=12$) and `GSE14502` ($n=87$), RMA-normalized $\log_2$ expression matrices were mapped to the 12 curated pathways and top 80 molecular hubs (`Table_S4A`, `Table_S4B`), computing baseline cell-type localization ($\Delta\log_2$ of each promoter line under normoxia minus the global normoxic mean across all lines) and cell-type hypoxia induction ($\Delta\log_2\text{FC}$ hypoxia minus normoxia within each promoter line). For the 2025 single-nucleus RNA-seq dataset `GSE308672` (`GSM9251057`–`GSM9251060`), we ingested the four 10x Genomics `.h5` count matrices (`raw_feature_bc_matrix.h5`), filtered barcodes to **40,860 high-quality nuclei** ($\ge 200$ detected genes and $\ge 300$ UMIs per nucleus), normalized counts to $\log_2(\text{CPM} + 1)$, assigned each nucleus to one of 9 canonical *Arabidopsis* root-tip lineages (`Stele_Pericycle`, `Phloem`, `Xylem`, `Endodermis`, `Cortex`, `Epidermis_Atrichoblast`, `Epidermis_Trichoblast`, `Columella_RootCap`, `Meristem_QC`) using established root marker gene signatures, and aggregated pseudobulk expression at the `sample × lineage` level (`wp3_gse308672_single_nucleus_lineage_counts.csv`, `Table_S4A`, `Table_S4B`).

### 4.5 Physiological, Metabolomic (`ZENODO16536514`), and Proteomic (`PXD002353`) Analyses (WP4)
From `ZENODO16536514`, we analyzed whole-plant gas exchange (`gas exchange.xlsx`, $n=52$), PAM chlorophyll fluorescence light-response curves (`chl fluorescence.xlsx`, $n=252$), leaf fresh/dry weight and relative water content (`RWC.xlsx`, $n=14$), foliar $\delta^{13}\text{C}$ and nitrogen content (`C_N_13C.xlsx`, $n=14$), pigment ratios (`Pigments.xlsx`, $n=20$), and OPLS-DA LC-MS secondary metabolites and galactolipids (`Secondary metabolites.xlsx`, 28 compounds across 4 pressure $\times$ humidity groups). Both parametric (`Mean`, `SD`, Welch's $t$-test $p$, Hedges' $g$) and non-parametric (`Median`, `IQR`, Mann–Whitney $U$ $p$) statistics were computed for every trait (`Table_S5A`, `Table_S5B`). For `PXD002353`, we parsed the three iTRAQ biological batch spreadsheets (`Batch 1.xlsx`, `Batch 2.xlsx`, `Batch 3.xlsx`, 5,454 unique AGI proteins) comparing wild-type `Col-0` and `SnRK1.1K48M` rosettes across $0, 0.5, 1,$ and $3\text{ h}$ of submergence, and integrated $\log_2$ protein ratios with our 283 transcriptomic hub nodes (`Table_S5C`).

### 4.6 Leave-One-Condition-Out Validation and Counterfactual Synthesis at NASA Setpoints (WP5)
To evaluate out-of-condition generalization across $(P_{\text{total}}, p\text{O}_2)$ space, we trained multi-output response-surface models (`TabPFN v2`, `TabPFN v2 (PHE)`, `Ridge`, `RandomForest`) mapping physical coordinates $[\text{HI}_{\text{baro}}, \text{HI}_{\text{hypo}}, D/D_0, F_{\text{I}}\text{O}_2, \mathbb{I}_{\text{Shoot}}, \text{Age}, \text{HI}_{\text{baro}}\times\mathbb{I}_{\text{Shoot}}, \text{HI}_{\text{hypo}}\times\mathbb{I}_{\text{Shoot}}]$ to differential expression ($\Delta\log_2\text{FC}$ vs. matched $97\text{ kPa}$ control) across the 12 pathways and 22 key hub genes (`src/atmos_ml/run_all_workpackages.py`). After validating out-of-condition accuracy via Leave-One-Condition-Out cross-validation (`Table_S6B`), we generated counterfactual predictions and ensemble uncertainty estimates ($\pm\text{SD}$ across seed/temperature configurations) at NASA's Exploration Habitat setpoint ($56.54\text{ kPa}, 34\%\ \text{O}_2$), Pre-EVA Staged Decompression setpoint ($70.33\text{ kPa}, 26.5\%\ \text{O}_2$), and Uncompensated $56.54\text{ kPa}$ Air ($21\%\ \text{O}_2$) (`Table_S6A`).
